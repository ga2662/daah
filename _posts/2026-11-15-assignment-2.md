# Mapping Occupational Diversity in Lorimer's Gazetteer

## Interactive 3D Map of Occupational Diversity

For our second Digital Humanities assignment, we explored the geographical distribution of occupations documented in J. G. Lorimer's *Gazetteer of the Persian Gulf, Oman and Central Arabia*. We organized the geographical locations and occupation categories into a dataset and used Kepler.gl to visualize patterns across the region.

The interactive map below uses 3D hexagonal columns to represent occupational diversity. The colors and heights indicate the average number of different occupations recorded among locations within each hexagonal area. Taller columns represent areas with greater recorded occupational diversity, while shorter columns represent areas with fewer documented occupations.

## About the Visualization

We created this visualization to explore how occupations were distributed across different geographical locations in Lorimer's Gazetteer. By using a 3D map, we wanted to make differences in recorded occupational diversity easier to identify and compare visually.

The map is interactive, allowing readers to zoom, rotate, and explore different parts of the region. However, the visualization reflects occupations documented in the historical source rather than a complete record of the occupations that existed in each location.
